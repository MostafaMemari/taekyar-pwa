## Remaining findings

1. **`text-ink-faint` used as real text — WCAG AA fail, both themes** (20 usages, 5 at 12px). `--ink-7` is `#d1d5db` (≈1.4:1 on light) and `#525d73` (≈2.4:1 on dark). Audit-001/002 fixed `--ink-6` only; the faint tier slipped through. Fix options: (a) point `--color-ink-faint` at an AA-safe step per theme (`--ink-5`) — collapses the faint/subtle distinction — or (b) move the 20 text usages to `text-ink-subtle` / `text-ink-muted` and reserve `ink-7` for non-text decoration. (b) is the honest one.
2. **`transition-[width]` on progress fills** ×2 (attendance-form.tsx:75, student-insurance-panel.tsx:57). The rulebook animates transform, not layout properties. At this bar size the stutter risk is small; if applied, switch to `scaleX` with `transform-origin: right` (RTL fill) or `left` per direction.
