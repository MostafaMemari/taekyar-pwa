# Audit 002 — taekyar-pwa

- Mode: **audit only** (report, no edits — user asked for a report)
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Scope: `src/**` (268 source files), tokens in `src/app/globals.css`
- Method: static reading + rule greps per the unslop checklist (`references/signs.md`). No browser render.
- Rulebook: `.agents/skills/unslop/SKILL.md` (unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, installed from github.com/heydamir/unslop; supersedes antislop v3.2.x used in audit-001)

## Verdict

**Light slop.** This app is mostly designed, not vibecoded: one real accent (crimson), a flat logo, zero emoji, zero fake claims or placeholder data, a real hero photo from the product's world, tabular figures, motion-safe entrance animations, single-accent charts with muted grids, and a belt motif used as a consistent signature. A Persian/RTL dojang identity exists and a name swap to another sport would not fit — the template check passes.

The remaining signs are dose and hygiene items, listed below.

## Findings (signs.md groups)

### Surfaces and color

1. **Colored glow dose on the welcome screen** — three sources on one screen: `role-card-hero` (globals.css:548 — `linear-gradient(135deg, ak-500, ak-700)` + inset white highlight + `44px` brand-colored shadow), the hero photo shadow `rgba(206,0,79,0.45)` (page.tsx:30), and the brand CTA variant `shadow-brand-xl` (button.tsx:11). The 135deg gradient is a tone shift inside one hue, which the rules allow; the white inner highlight + colored shadow combo is the flagged "glass card" treatment.
2. **Accent radial washes** — globals.css:479 and :488 (7–10% accent radials) and :678 (dark coach band corner wash at 34%). The dark band is the app's one rich moment, so the 34% wash is defensible; the two small radials are decorative filler.
3. **Hue gradients on accent surfaces** — club header banner `from-brand-600 to-brand-400` (club-info-card.tsx:11) and attendance progress fill `from-brand-400 to-brand-600` (attendance-form.tsx:75). Small same-hue shifts, but combined with the glow shadow on the CTA the accent reads "glowy" more than "deliberate".

### Type

4. **Hierarchy carried by weight, not size** — `font-extrabold` (800) on 66 occurrences including 11–13px labels (students-list-item, filters sheet, buttons). Persian UI tolerates heavier weights than Latin, and 700 bold is a deliberate identity here; but 800 at 12px on every label is the "every element highlighted" sign in type form. 400/500/700 (600–700 for emphasis) with size+color hierarchy would read more designed.

### Spacing

5. **Off-grid heights** — `h-[82px]` (invitations-list.tsx), `h-[74px]` (dashboard-attendance.tsx, attendance-form-skeleton.tsx). Everything else sampled sits on the 4px scale; `py-[3px]` nudges are allowed.

### Copy

6. **Em dash in UI copy — 28 occurrences, unchanged since audit-001** (page.tsx, verify-otp-form, coach-signup-form, student-signup-step-documents, settings screens, dashboard-overview placeholder, coach-profile-form, security-screen, student attendance/insurance panels). Persian copy should use «،» / «؛» / parentheses; the `"—"` placeholders should become a skeleton or «ثبت نشده».
7. Clean: no exclamation marks in interface text, no greeting headlines (the page H1 is the product name), no hype words, no unverified claims, no `[X]`/`123,456` fake data.

### Components

8. Clean: logo mark is flat (no gradient, no glow, no sparkle — audit-001's finding is resolved), status pills are quiet (no glow), charts use one accent + muted grid + small radii (dashboard-attendance), `rounded-full` count (112) is dominated by legitimate circles (avatars, belt dots, radio/check circles).

### Motion

9. **`animate-ping` without `motion-safe`** — page.tsx:17 welcome status dot. Flagged in audit-001 (R-19), still unfixed.
10. **`hover:scale-110`** — theme-switch.tsx:39 (18px icon; small, but the rule prefers color/surface steps).
11. **`transition-all`** — badge.tsx, button.tsx, input-otp.tsx, tabs.tsx, toggle.tsx (shadcn scaffold primitives). LOW: name the properties (`transition-colors` etc.).
12. Clean: skeleton `animate-pulse` is legitimate loading state; page entrances are `motion-safe:` guarded fade+rise, one pattern.

### Accessibility-adjacent (carried over from audit-001's hard gates)

13. **`--ink-6: #9ca3af` still fails WCAG AA as text** (globals.css:137) — 2.6:1 on white, used as text in 100+ places at 12px (`text-ink-subtle`). HIGH priority, one-token fix (~`#667085`).
14. **Async state still barely announced** — only 3 `aria-busy`/`aria-live` uses across 148+ skeleton renderings.

### Not covered here

- `DESIGN.md` with the direction dials still does not exist (audit-001 R-37) — the direction section below is written to be lifted into it.

## Direction (proposed — decide before any edit)

- **Product.** A coach's companion for running a taekwondo club: students, belts, insurance, classes, attendance.
- **Character.** Disciplined, exact, quietly ceremonial.
- **World.** The dojang — training-hall dark, mats, the rank ceremony.
- **Moments.** (1) The dark coach band with its corner accent wash — the evening hall. (2) Rank itself: belt strips and glyphs wherever rank appears, and the one bright brand role-card on the welcome screen.
- **Signature.** The belt strip/glyph — already used consistently; keep it exclusive to rank contexts.

## Suggested fixes if applied (smallest set, in order)

1. `--ink-6` → `#667085` (one token; keep `#9ca3af` for non-text).
2. Replace the 28 em dashes with Persian punctuation; `"—"` placeholders → skeleton/«ثبت نشده».
3. `motion-safe:` on the welcome ping; drop `hover:scale-110`; name the `transition-all`s in ui primitives.
4. Glow dose: make the hero photo shadow neutral; keep exactly one brand glow (role-card-hero) or flatten it to a solid `ak-600` surface — pick one story for the accent.
5. `h-[82px]`/`h-[74px]` → 80/72 or 76 (4px scale).
6. Write `DESIGN.md` from the direction above (identity, palette, type, radius, shadow, dials).
7. `aria-busy` on the skeleton→content swap containers; one `role="status"` for empty states.
